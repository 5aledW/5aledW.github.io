Track your shows. Talk about every episode.

TrackerTV is a free TV show tracker for iPhone. Keep track of every episode you watch, see what to watch next, get a notification when a new episode comes out, and join the discussion under each episode with other fans.

Frequently asked questions

How do I mark an episode as watched?
On the Shows tab, tap the round check next to a show, or swipe the show to the left. You can also open any show and tick episodes one by one, or tap “Mark season as watched”.

How do I get new-episode notifications?
Open the Upcoming tab and tap “Turn on”, or go to Profile → ⚙️ Settings → New episode alerts. If you said no before, allow notifications for TrackerTV in the iPhone Settings app.

Can I bring my TV Time history?
Yes. If you have the data export from TV Time, go to Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Import from TV Time and choose the .csv files from the export.

How do I report a comment or block someone?
Press and hold a comment, or tap the ⋯ next to it, then choose Report or Block. Reported comments are reviewed by a moderator, usually within 24 hours. You can unblock people in Settings → Blocked people.

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, watch history, lists and comments. If you can’t access the app, email us from the address you signed up with and we’ll delete it for you.

Community rules

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats or hate towards anyone.
  • Respect spoilers. Talk about an episode under that episode.
  • Keep it clean. No sexual, violent or illegal content, and no spam.
  • Zero tolerance. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and people who keep breaking them lose their account.
