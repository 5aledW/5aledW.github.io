Privacy policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the TrackerTV app (“TrackerTV”, “we”) collects, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, email trackertv.app1@gmail.com.

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password (used only to sign you in), username, and optionally a display name, bio and profile photo.
  • What you track: the shows you add, the episodes you mark as watched, your ratings, favorites, lists, reactions (“How did you feel?”) and “best character” votes.
  • What you post: comments and replies, likes, the people you follow, and reports or blocks you make.
  • Files you choose: if you import your TV Time history, the app reads the files you pick on your phone and saves the matched shows and episodes to your account. The files themselves are not uploaded.

What other people can see

TrackerTV is a social app. Other signed-in users can see your username, display name, bio, profile photo, comments, lists, the shows you track, the episodes you watched and your follower counts. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Advertising

TrackerTV is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address) and information about how you interact with ads.

  • The app asks for your permission (Apple’s “Allow tracking” prompt) before ads can use your advertising identifier. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, you still see ads, but they are less personalized.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app first asks for your consent through Google’s consent form.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Notifications

New-episode alerts are scheduled on your phone itself. We don’t collect a push notification token or any information about your notifications.

How we use information

  • To run the app: your account, your tracked shows, your lists, the social features and the comment threads.
  • To keep the community safe: reviewing reported comments and enforcing the community rules.
  • To show ads that keep the app free (see above).

We do not sell your personal information.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your account and app data on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Google AdMob provides the ads.
  • TVmaze provides show and episode information. Your phone requests this information directly from TVmaze, so TVmaze can see your IP address. We don’t send TVmaze anything about your account.

How long we keep information

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, watch history, lists, comments, likes, follows and profile photo are permanently deleted from our database right away.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Delete account.
  • Edit your profile in Profile → Edit profile, and remove shows, comments or lists in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask any other question about your information, by emailing trackertv.app1@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU or UK), you may have extra rights, such as objecting to how your data is used or complaining to your local data protection authority.

Children

TrackerTV is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
